# Air Visual Card

A modern, responsive Home Assistant Lovelace card that displays Air Quality Index (AQI), pollution levels, and weather data in the style of AirVisual.

## Features

- **Modern & Classic Layouts**: Choose between the new Modern redesign or traditional Classic layout.
- **Bi-Directional EPA AQI & Pollutant Calculations**: Automatically computes AQI from raw pollutant concentrations ($PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$, $O_3$, etc.), or computes concentration from AQI when numeric sensors are missing.
- **Integration Presets**: One-click configuration with auto-detection for Google Air Quality, Tomorrow.io, openSenseMap, OpenWeatherMap, AirVisual (IQAir), and WAQI.
- **Dynamic Theming**: Card colors and face graphics dynamically adjust across all 6 official EPA AQI hazard tiers.
- **100% Self-Contained**: Vector face and weather SVGs are bundled directly into the card.
- **Custom Title**: Clean custom title without prefixes or suffixes.
- **Full Action Handling**: Native tap, hold, and double-tap gestures with more-info dialog support.
- **Sections Dashboard Ready**: Full support for Home Assistant's new Sections dashboard grid.
